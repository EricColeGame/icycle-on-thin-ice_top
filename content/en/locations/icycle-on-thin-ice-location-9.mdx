### Phase 1: The Initial Descent and Ramp Launch

1. **Controlled Roll-In:** Tap backwards slightly as you drop into the initial slope to calibrate your wheel balance.

## Pro Tips for Advanced Momentum and Surface Control

Refining Dennis's eccentric physics engine is what separates casual completion from high-tier speedrunning. Here are several actionable techniques proven by player experience to optimize runs:

- **The Micro-Hop Deceleration:** Normal braking causes Dennis to skid unpredictably on sheer ice. Instead of pulling hard in reverse, execute rapid, micro-hops while tapping the opposite direction to shave off speed without entering a spin.
- **Canopy Feathering:** Never hold your glide continuously across open expanses. Pulsing the canopy twice per second keeps Dennis's vertical profile level and prevents floating into high hitboxes.
- **Surface Compression Jump:** When landing on downhill slopes, time your jump command precisely upon contact. Dennis will absorb the downward force and launch forward with nearly double the horizontal velocity of a flat leap.
- **Visual Cue Calibration:** Watch Dennis's posture. When his bike frame tilts past a 45-degree angle backwards, immediate forward compensation is required to avoid flipping completely during landing.

Applying these principles consistently allows you to navigate the narrow ledges of **icycle on thin ice location 9** with poise and precision, turning frantic scrambles into graceful, cinematic runs.
