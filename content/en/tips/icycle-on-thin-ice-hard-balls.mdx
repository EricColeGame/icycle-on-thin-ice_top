## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most effective way to dodge icycle on thin ice hard balls on steep slopes?
The most reliable technique is to avoid outrunning the ball down a continuous decline. Instead, execute a controlled hop using your umbrella to hang in the air, allowing the rolling sphere to pass underneath your wheels before landing safely on the slope behind it.

### Do cosmetic items change Dennis's hitboxes against rolling hazards?
No. All cosmetic suits, hats, and novelty accessories in the game share the exact same underlying character hitbox. Dennis will interact with physical hazards, falling debris, and rolling spheres in the exact same manner regardless of his attire.

### How do I collect ice drops located directly beneath rolling spheres?
Do not attempt to snatch these items while the hazard is actively rolling. Bait the sphere by tapping the trigger line, retreat to a safe alcove or elevated platform, and let the ball crash into a barrier or roll off-screen. Once the path is clear, return to collect the remaining items safely.
