3. **The Hairpin Catch**: As you touch down on the lower ledge, immediately tap backward to counter excess speed, preventing Dennis from sliding into the downward ice spike.

This downward acceleration converts into forward glide velocity, skimming cleanly underneath the ceiling hazards and over the abyss.
