### Phase 2: The Vertical Chasm and Updraft Tunnel

1. Stop just before the threshold of the shaft to trigger the first set of falling icicles harmlessly.
2. Pedal into the thermal updraft with Dennis's umbrella open to catch the vertical lift.
3. Tilt slightly forward to avoid the jagged left-hand wall as you rise.
4. Release the umbrella near the top of the draft to snap out of the float and land firmly on the solid ledge to your right.

### Phase 3: The Fractured Run and Final Ascent

1. Pump the forward pedal rhythmically down the jagged terrain to maintain control without spinning out.
2. Jump across the three short floating ice blocks, tapping your glide briefly between hops to extend hang time.
3. As the final incline appears, stay committed to full acceleration to clear the last crevasse and hit the completion marker.
