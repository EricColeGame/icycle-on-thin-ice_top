### Micro-Bouncing and Tire Hopping
When landing on a downward slope, holding the forward directional key preserves your accumulated speed. Tapping jump precisely at the moment of contact produces a "micro-bounce." This cancels Dennis's heavy landing recovery animation, launching him forward with no loss of forward drive.

### The Umbrella Stall
During stages with falling icicles or moving crushers, maintaining air position is essential. Deploying your umbrella halts downward velocity briefly, while immediately collapsing it drops Dennis straight down like an anchor. Alternating rapidly between open and closed positions allows you to hover in place, letting lethal hazards pass harmlessly beneath your wheels.

### Corner Canceling
When approaching steep, ramp-like hills, initiate a jump just before reaching the ramp's incline. Dennis will skim low across the surface rather than launching into a wide, floaty arc. This low-profile trajectory protects you from overhead hazards common in advanced secret gauntlets.
