## Frequently Asked Questions About Icycle on Thin Ice Window Shopping

### Is icycle on thin ice window shopping purely cosmetic, or do items affect gameplay?
While the majority of wardrobe pieces like hats, suits, and beards are cosmetic, specific items such as umbrellas and collection charms directly affect gameplay physics. Upgraded umbrellas provide extended glide hang-time and softer descents, making difficult platforming sections much more forgiving.

### What is the fastest way to earn ice for store purchases?
The most efficient method is replaying early dream sequences that feature wide collection arcs and low death penalties. Stages with gentle slopes allow you to sweep up large clusters of ice chips without worrying about sudden crushing hazards or disintegrating platforms.

### Can I change Dennis's outfit at any time during a level?
No, wardrobe changes must be made in the closet hub between levels or from the stage selection menu. Once Dennis starts pedaling in an active stage, his equipped outfit and umbrella remain locked until you complete or exit the course.

### Do cosmetic items change Dennis's collision box?
Player experience indicates that cosmetic items do not alter Dennis's core collision mechanics. However, oversized hats or large back items can make it harder to see falling obstacles, so many players prefer compact accessories during tight, hazard-heavy levels.
