## Step-by-Step Walkthrough: Conquering the Hazards

Tackling this stage requires dividing the course into distinct segments. Memorizing each transition allows you to anticipate incoming hazards rather than reacting to off-screen dangers.

### Phase 1: The Initial Descent and Gap Clears

The run begins with an immediate downhill slope. Holding forward without caution will launch Dennis straight into a ceiling hazard. Instead, allow momentum to carry you midway down the slope before executing a short hop across the first crevasse.

Once airborne, tap your umbrella control briefly to dampen your descent speed. Landing smoothly on the first plateau requires landing flat on both wheels; hitting the ice at an awkward angle will toss Dennis off his unicycle, prompting a checkpoint reload.

### Phase 2: The Mid-Stage Wind Tunnel and Falling Debris

After clearing the initial ledges, you encounter the wind tunnel section. This is widely considered the technical chokepoint of **Icycle on Thin Ice Level 26**. Powerful currents blow upward while icicles plummet from the ceiling. 

To clear this corridor cleanly:
1. Approach the updraft with moderate speed rather than full acceleration.
2. Open the umbrella as soon as you enter the wind stream to gain safe vertical clearance.
3. Watch the ceiling shadow indicators to identify where icicles detach.
4. Close the umbrella intermittently to drop underneath falling spikes before opening it again to catch the secondary draft.

### Phase 3: Precision Float and the Final Stretch

The final stretch demands strict throttle control. Players must bounce across a series of buoyant items suspended over an endless abyss. Maintain steady horizontal pressure and resist the urge to deploy the umbrella continuously, as doing so robs you of necessary forward velocity.
