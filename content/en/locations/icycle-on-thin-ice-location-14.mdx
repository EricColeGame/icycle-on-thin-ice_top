Once you cross the initial drop, the stage transforms into a low-gravity aerial sequence. Here, Dennis must rely heavily on his trusty umbrella to bridge wide voids between isolated platforms.

When traversing large chasms, many players make the mistake of leaving the umbrella deployed continuously. This results in slow horizontal travel, leaving Dennis exposed to descending debris. By rapidly toggling the glide button—deploying the canopy for half a second, canceling it into a brief freefall, and deploying it again—you preserve forward momentum while keeping a flat, efficient trajectory.
